Our Story

Founded in 2019, ShopEase started with a simple mission: make quality products accessible to everyone.

How It Started

ShopEase was born out of frustration with complicated, expensive e-commerce platforms. Our founders — two engineers from San Francisco — wanted to build something simpler.

What started as a weekend project in a garage quickly grew into a platform serving customers across 50+ countries. We remain committed to our founding principle: quality products, fair prices, fast delivery.
